Support and enquiries

Mac Storage Guardian Pro is preparing for public release. For product enquiries, feature questions, or early feedback, use the public project contact route below.

Contact route

Open a product enquiry on GitHub. A free GitHub account may be required. Please do not post personal, financial, confidential, or sensitive file information in a public issue.

Current availability

There is no live checkout, downloadable retail installer, paid activation, or subscription service on this informational site yet. Pricing, compatibility, and customer support details will be provided when the product is released.

Need a safety clarification?

The product is designed to use explicit permissions for operations that may release original files. Verify destinations and keep separate backups before making changes to important files.
