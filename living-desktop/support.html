Support and enquiries

Living Desktop is preparing for public release. For pre-release product questions or feedback, use the public project contact route below.

Public contact route

Open a Living Desktop product enquiry on GitHub. A free GitHub account may be required. Please do not post personal, financial, confidential, account, license-key, or sensitive file information in a public issue.

What retail support will cover

  • installation and activation issues;
  • startup and cold-reopen problems;
  • reference-based Fences, Folder Portal, Scenes, Quick Shelf, Today and Post-it behavior;
  • supported language and accessibility issues;
  • safe uninstall and recovery questions;
  • reproducible product defects.

Local-first diagnostics

Living Desktop does not upload diagnostics automatically. If troubleshooting is necessary, you may be asked to provide a version number, Windows version, screenshots, and logs or receipts that you choose to share.

Current availability

There is no live checkout, public retail installer, paid activation or subscription service on this informational site yet. Commercial support details will be finalized with the signed release and approved storefront.
