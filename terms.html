No sales are being made on this site

This is a product information site. Mac Storage Guardian Pro is not yet being sold or licensed to customers through this site. No checkout, payment collection, or contract is offered here.

Planned pricing (not yet available to purchase)

The intended launch plans are US$9.99 per year for a one-Mac subscription, or US$49.99 once for perpetual use of the purchased V1 major version on one Mac. The subscription is intended to include available updates during its active term; future major-version upgrades may be sold separately to one-time buyers. No purchase or automatic renewal can currently be made through this site.

Future purchases

If commercial sales open, an approved checkout provider will identify the merchant, applicable currency and taxes, subscription renewal details, refund and cancellation terms, and payment options before purchase. Do not submit payment details to this informational website.
