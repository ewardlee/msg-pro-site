Designed with safety in mind

Your files are worth more than a quick cleanup.

MSG Pro is designed to keep the decision to remove an original distinct from verifying its archived copy. The workflow emphasizes visible status, meaningful history, and recovery choices.

What to know before launch

Is MSG Pro available to buy now?

No. The public commercial release is being prepared. This website is an informational preview, not an active storefront.

Does it automatically delete my files?

MSG Pro is designed around reviewable actions and explicit authorization before removing originals. Its archive workflow prioritizes verification and recovery information.

Which platform is it for?

Mac computers running macOS. Final compatibility details will be published with the release.
